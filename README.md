# World Weird Food Tour — Guide to the World's Strangest Foods

Short, sourced guides to unusual foods from around the world, from the **World Weird Food Tour** YouTube channel. Every food below has a full documentary episode: its history, how it is made, and how the people who grew up with it actually eat it. No fake tasting, no invented shops or prices — just the real story of each food.

## The foods

### 1. Century Egg (Pidan) — China
A duck or chicken egg preserved in a mixture of clay, ash, salt, and lime for weeks to months. The white turns into a dark amber jelly and the yolk becomes creamy and deep green-grey. A delicacy with a history stretching back centuries, eaten in congee, with tofu, or simply sliced with pickled ginger.
**Watch:** https://youtu.be/wC9pw2-XEtY

### 2. Durian — Southeast Asia
The king of fruits: a large, spiky fruit whose powerful smell gets it banned from hotels and public transport across Southeast Asia, while its custard-like flesh has devoted fans across Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, and the Philippines.
**Watch:** https://youtu.be/Uf_vfZ8JHmM

### 3. Chocolate — from bitter ritual drink to sweet bar
Long before it was a sweet snack, cacao was a bitter, spiced drink at the heart of Mesoamerican ceremony. The episode follows how chocolate crossed oceans, changed form, and became the world's favourite treat.
**Watch:** https://youtu.be/b3aGc5vrL-s

### 4. Hákarl — Iceland
Greenland shark, fermented and hung to dry for months. The fresh meat is poisonous, so Icelanders developed a preservation method that turns it into a storied national food — famous today for its powerful ammonia smell and its role as a rite of passage for visitors.
**Watch:** https://youtu.be/V02YegFSmY0

### 5. Surströmming — Sweden
Baltic herring fermented in barrels and sold in cans that swell as the fermentation continues. Opened outdoors, traditionally eaten in late summer with flatbread, potatoes, onion, and sour cream. One of the strongest-smelling foods on Earth — and a proud Swedish tradition.
**Watch:** https://youtu.be/Le1b7Phu2ZY
